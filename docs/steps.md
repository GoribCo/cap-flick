# Ice Cream Cone Dice Game: Steps

Source: `docs/games.mp4` (Facebook reel 1665643661733233, about 2 minutes).
Steps are taken from the video frames. The audio was not transcribed.

## What you need

- A sheet of paper.
- One six-sided die.
- One marker per player, in different colours (the video uses purple and orange).

## Setup

1. Draw one ice cream cone per player, facing each player's side of the paper.
2. Above each cone, draw a stack of 8 empty scoops (circles).
3. Give each player a marker in their own colour.

## How to play

1. Players take turns rolling the die.
2. On the first turn, write the number you rolled in the scoop closest to your cone.
3. On every later turn, add your roll to the number in your last scoop.
4. Write the new total in the next empty scoop up the stack.
5. Keep taking turns until every scoop on both stacks is filled.

## Winning

1. Compare the top scoop of each stack. That is each player's final total.
2. The higher total wins.
3. Circle the winning total in a highlight colour and draw a star next to the winner's cone.
4. Shake hands.

## Notes

- The child's first scoop is 9, which is more than one die can show.
  The video does not make clear whether the first turn uses two rolls added together.
- The game practises adding a single digit to a running total.
